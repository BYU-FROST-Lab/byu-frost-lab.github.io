Damon is a graduate student in the Computer Science program. He received a BS in Computer Science from Brigham Young University in April 2024. As an undergraduate, Damon studied computational biophysics, specifically deep neural networks for computational drug discovery and biophysical simulation. As a graduate student, Damon's research focuses on the intersection of formal methods, planning, controls, and machine learning for autonomous systems, specifically how we can assure autonomous systems performance and behaviour.

Damon grew up in Stuttgart, Germany. In his free time he enjoys skiing, playing music, reading, and playing with his three kids.
